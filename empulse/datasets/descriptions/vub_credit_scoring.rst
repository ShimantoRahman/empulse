VUB Credit Scoring Dataset

Anonymized credit scoring dataset from a Romanian non-banking financial institution (NBFI),
provided by the VUB Data Analytics Laboratory (Petrides et al., 2020).
The goal is to predict whether a borrower will experience 45+ days of payment delay (default).

=================   ==============
Classes                          2
Defaulters                    3206
Non-defaulters               15711
Samples                      18917
Features                        16
=================   ==============
